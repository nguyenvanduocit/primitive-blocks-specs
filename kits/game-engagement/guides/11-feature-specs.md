# Feature Specs

One behaviour spec for each feature a mobile game needs: the need it serves, the rules and defaults, the states that fail in production, the events it emits, what it depends on and the block that implements it. Guide 10 (`10-screens-and-navigation.md`) says where each feature appears on screen; guide 12 (`12-live-ops-and-instrumentation.md`) holds the event envelope and the live-ops process.

Evidence: platform rules come from the Apple App Review Guidelines and the Apple, Android and Google Play documentation and policy pages, read on 2026-10-27 and labelled HIGH. Design figures come from the sources named in guides 01 to 06. A value no source settles is called "kit default": a starting point to measure, not a finding. Event names come from the block README named in a feature header or from the core table of guide 12; names after "proposed" have no owning schema yet.

## Decision rule

1. **One shape for every feature, and cut features before edge cases.** Each spec has Serves (hook, rhythm, stay, objective, earn, safety or reach), Rules, Bites (the states that fail: offline, clock, multi-device, refund, guest to account) and Events. A feature ships with its Bites handled or not at all (kit recommendation, MEDIUM).
2. **Pay out through one ledger and time through one clock.** Every currency or item reward and every purchase grant calls the ledger contract of `billing.virtual-currency` with an idempotency key built from stable facts, with a line suffix when one claim pays several lines (`daily-streak:{account_id}:{day_key}:{line_no}`). Each paying feature registers its reasons as `currency_flows` rows, and the ledger rejects a reason it lacks (`unknown_reason`; reasons registry in section 6 of `blocks/billing/virtual-currency/README.md`); a game's own payouts (tutorial, level rewards, ads, referral) register theirs the same way. Energy is a meter outside the ledger, credited through `grantEnergy` with the ledger's shape and its own idempotency (`engagement.energy-lives`). Every timer, window and day boundary comes from the server (`server_now`, `ends_at`, `day_key`). Many Bites below then reduce to a replay returning the original result (kit contract, MEDIUM as design).
3. **Money and chance carry store rules.** Purchased currency may not expire and restorable purchases need a restore path ([Apple 3.1.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH). Random items sold for money need their odds disclosed before purchase (Apple 3.1.1, HIGH; [Google Play Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738), section 7: "in advance of" and "in close and timely proximity to" the purchase, HIGH). An auto-renewing subscription needs ongoing value, a period of at least a week and availability on all the user's devices ([Apple 3.1.2(a)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH).
4. **Identity, chat and permissions carry store rules.** An app without significant account-based features must work without a login, and account creation requires in-app deletion ([Apple 5.1.1(v)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH). User-generated content needs filtering, reporting, blocking and published contact ([Apple 1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH; [Google Play UGC policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937), HIGH). An app may not require push, tracking or other system permissions for access or compensation ([Apple 5.1.2(i)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH).
5. **Take the feature set from the interview, not from a top-grossing list.** Prevalence counts show what ships in top games (daily login in 38 of 40, loot boxes in 37, battle passes in 31) in one country and a sample skewed to gacha RPG and strategy ([arXiv 2504.10714](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.10714), MEDIUM). The matrix below marks each feature per archetype and the build order sets when it ships. No source gives a measured lift for any feature (see Gaps), so each spec states behaviour and the event that measures it.

## Feature specs

1. **Tutorial and first win** · hook · no block yet · needs a guest account (25), the ledger for step rewards, events
   - Rules: one mechanic per step; the first win lands inside the first couple of minutes; a small reward per step; three goals (short, medium, long) at the end; no account, ad, paywall, rating or permission ask before the first win (guide 01, MEDIUM). Offer a skip to returning players, since a long forced tutorial drives experienced players away ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/16-reasons-why-players-are-leaving-your-game), MEDIUM).
   - Bites: a kill mid-step resumes at the last completed step; a reinstall or second device does not replay a finished tutorial; each step reward is granted once under the key `tutorial:{account_id}:{step_id}:{line_no}` and a registered reason; the tutorial is identical for every player so the funnel stays readable ([Supersonic](https://supersonic.com/learn/blog/optimizing-ftue/), MEDIUM).
   - Events: `tutorial_step`, `level_complete` (guide 12); `starter_objectives_completed` (`engagement.objectives`).
2. **Core loop and level progression** · stay · no block yet (`data.progress-sync` stores results) · needs 1, ledger
   - Rules: the four layers of guide 01 (action with instant feedback, reward per cycle, a visible next goal, a reason to return); low early XP thresholds so the first level-ups arrive fast ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding), MEDIUM, platform-specific). Curves and unlock cadence belong to guide 08; no source settles them (Gaps).
   - Bites: reference levels by stable id, never list position, so a content update cannot move a player; offline play records results locally and syncs on reconnect; scores that feed boards or pay rewards are device-reported, so the server bounds them as `ugc.friends-leaderboard` does; a paid continue takes the spend and grants the continue in one step.
   - Events: `session_start`, `level_start`, `level_complete`, `level_fail` (guide 12).
3. **Daily reward and streak** · rhythm · `engagement.daily-streak` · needs ledger, clock
   - Rules: one claim per `day_key`; `CLAIM_REQUIREMENT` is `play_to_claim` by default, so the reward pays for play (a daily jackpot won through levels, [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/live-ops-trends-powering-mobile-puzzle/), HIGH) and `open_app` is the weaker choice; `MISS_POLICY` is `reset`, `decay` or `freeze_protected` (default), the loss-avoidance lever, so choose it deliberately (guide 06). The one measured streak test is Duolingo's, an education app: separating the daily goal from the streak raised relative day-14 retention by 3.3% ([Duolingo, 2020](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak), HIGH as reported).
   - Bites: the device clock changes nothing and a timezone change is rate-limited by the block; a claim started before midnight that arrives after it takes the `day_key` the server computes on arrival; two devices claim once per account and day and the second receives the original result; each reward line has its own ledger key (`:{line_no}`); offline shows the last known state with claim disabled; a guest merge runs through the block's `mergeAccounts`.
   - Events: `daily_reward_popup_shown`, `daily_reward_claimed`, `streak_extended`, `streak_lost`, `streak_freeze_used`, `streak_repair_accepted`.
4. **Objectives (starter, daily, weekly, achievements)** · objective · `engagement.objectives` · needs ledger, clock, 1
   - Rules: starter objectives close onboarding; daily objectives fit one normal session; weekly ones belong to the weekly layer; achievements are permanent and optional. Daily missions are a recurring-event layer in top-grossing games ([Sensor Tower, March 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/top-grossing-mobile-games-live-ops-strategies-2025-report), HIGH). A finished objective never expires: `UNCLAIMED_AT_RESET` is `stay_claimable` (default) or `auto_grant`, and the screen says which (INV-9). Ladders and sizing belong to guide 08.
   - Bites: progress counts in the period where the action happened, by server time; a completion earned offline is granted once on sync under `objectives:{account_id}:{objective_id}:{window_key}:{line_index}`; completion pays nothing and only a claim calls the ledger (INV-7); a ledger rejection leaves the objective completed and claimable; an objective removed by an update keeps the reward stored on the instance.
   - Events: `objective_assigned`, `objective_progress`, `objective_completed`, `objective_claimed`, `objective_expired`.
5. **Energy or lives** · rhythm · `engagement.energy-lives` · needs ledger (paid refills), clock, 8
   - Rules: cap, regeneration interval and cost per attempt are tunables with an owner. Energy is a meter and not a ledger currency: `daily-streak`, `season-pass`, `event-calendar`, `objectives` and the ad route credit it through `grantEnergy`, and only a paid refill is a ledger `spend` (`energy-lives.refill`). Royal Match hands out unlimited lives as an event reward ([Sensor Tower, May 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/what-royal-matchs-calendar-teaches-about-live-ops-in-2026), HIGH). A fan wiki puts Candy Crush at a life per half hour up to five, an example only ([Candy Crush Wiki](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Lives), LOW). A 2022 heuristic evaluation names timers and energy "Playing by Appointment" and "Pay to Skip" dark patterns ([Dahlan and Susanty, 28 Nov 2022](https://www.deceptive.design/articles/finding-dark-patterns-in-casual-mobile-games-using-heuristic-evaluation), MEDIUM), so the wait and the price are stated where they apply.
   - Bites: regeneration is computed from the last balance and `server_now`; regeneration stops at the cap; one value per meter and account across devices; a crash between spend and attempt start costs nothing; a refill the meter cannot deliver is refunded to the player's currency (`energy-lives.refill-refund`).
   - Events: `energy_spent`, `energy_refilled` (with source), `energy_empty_shown`.
6. **Currencies and the shop** · earn · `billing.virtual-currency` (`billing.in-app-purchase` for a one-time unlock) · needs ledger, 25
   - Rules: a soft currency earned by play and a hard currency mostly bought, each with named faucets and sinks (guide 03; [Adrian Crook, 2016](https://adriancrook.com/?p=4918), MEDIUM); every currency and item grant or spend goes through the ledger; prices come from the store in the player's currency and show in real money wherever currency is sold (guide 06); real-money spend stays off until the host's `purchase_policy` verifies an adult (guide 15); a one-time unlock stays with `billing.in-app-purchase` (interview 2).
   - Bites: a completed purchase whose grant failed is granted once on retry from the purchase record (`store_transaction_id`); refunds arrive as an App Store [`REFUND` notification](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreservernotifications/notificationtype) (HIGH) or a Google Play [voided purchase](https://developers.google.com/android-publisher/voided-purchases) (HIGH), and the block decides what happens to currency already spent because no source settles it; `insufficient_balance` opens the shop with the exact shortfall.
   - Events: `currency_grant`, `currency_spend`, `purchase_verified`, `purchase_refunded` (guide 12); `shop_viewed`, `purchase_started`, `purchase_failed`, `purchase_blocked`, `ledger_rejected`.
7. **Starter pack and offers** · earn · no block yet (purchase contract of `billing.virtual-currency`) · needs 6, clock
   - Rules: one cheap, time-boxed first purchase shown after the first win and a felt need, then bundles above it (guide 03 rule 4, MEDIUM). Rolling offers, linked bundles claimed in sequence and often opening free, are a top-game pattern ([Sensor Tower, March 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/top-grossing-mobile-games-live-ops-strategies-2025-report), HIGH). A piggy bank that fills with play and opens for a price is a documented variant ([Heroic Labs](https://heroiclabs.com/blog/understanding-piggy-bank-mechanics-mobile-games/), MEDIUM, no figures).
   - Bites: expiry is the server `ends_at` and a timer never restarts (guide 15, RED); a purchase begun before expiry is honoured (kit default); a one-time offer bought on one device disappears on all, keyed by `store_transaction_id`; countdowns show only where `purchase_policy` sets `countdown_offers_allowed`, since EU consumer authorities list time-limited offers as a pressure tactic (guide 06, MEDIUM); refunds follow 6.
   - Events: `offer_shown` (guide 12), `pack_selected`, `purchase_verified`, `purchase_refunded`.
8. **Rewarded ads** · earn · no block yet · needs ledger, 5 or 6 as the blocked moment
   - Rules: opt-in only, placed where the player is blocked: out of lives, failed level, short of currency ([Unity/ironSource](https://unity.com/blog/3-ways-to-optimize-the-ad-experience-for-your-users), MEDIUM). Apple allows incentivizing an ad view inside the app ([Apple 3.2.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH). The Kids Category should carry no third-party analytics or advertising, with narrow contextual-advertising exceptions ([Apple 1.3](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH), and iOS tracking needs App Tracking Transparency permission (Apple 5.1.2(i), HIGH). That viewers retain and pay more is correlational (guide 03, LOW as causal).
   - Bites: the reward follows the network's server-to-server callback, verified by the game's ad route, with a key built from the network's transaction id (`energy-lives:{account_id}:ad:{tx}` for energy via `grantEnergy`, a ledger `grant` for currency); no fill or a failed load hides the offer and never shows a dead button; preload, since latency is a common cause of bad rewarded UX ([Unity/ironSource, 2022](https://unity.com/blog/understanding-the-impact-of-rewarded-ads-on-iap-retention-and-engagement), MEDIUM); a kill during playback grants nothing unless the callback arrived; the offer is hidden offline.
   - Events: `ad_shown`, `ad_rewarded`, `error` (guide 12); `energy_refilled` with source `ad`.
9. **Subscription or VIP** · stay · `billing.subscriptions` · needs `billing.virtual-currency` (purchase record and store receivers), ledger for stipends
   - Rules: ongoing value, a period of at least a week and availability on all the user's devices (Apple 3.1.2(a), HIGH); benefits (ad removal, a daily stipend, pass access) are read through `entitlement(account_id, product_code)`, computed on the server from verified store state (INV-1), never from store state or the client. The paywall shows plan, period, full renewal price, what is included, how to cancel, restore and the terms before the store sheet opens (INV-14), and asks nothing extra of a subscriber (INV-15). No usable data on game subscriptions exists (guide 03 Gaps).
   - Bites: states are `pending`, `trial`, `active`, `canceled`, `grace`, `retry`, `paused`, `expired`, `revoked`, and each needs its own copy; grace entitles and retry, pause and expiry do not (INV-5); a cancelled subscription keeps access until `expires_at` (INV-6); a stipend slice is granted once, for paid terms only (INV-10, `subscriptions.stipend`); an account with an entitled chain cannot buy a second one in the group (INV-18); a restore that finds a chain on another account asks before it transfers; a refund or voided purchase ends the entitlement and may reverse the stipend.
   - Events: `subscription_paywall_viewed`, `subscription_purchase_succeeded`, `subscription_restore_completed`, `subscription_stipend_granted`, `subscription_refund_processed`.
10. **Season pass** · stay · `engagement.season-pass` · needs ledger, 11 (season window), 4 (progress source), purchase contract
    - Rules: a free and a premium track over a month-scale season, with premium rewards visible to non-buyers ([GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/designing-battle-passes-in-mobile-games-the-whats-whys-and-hows), MEDIUM; vendor price medians are LOW and unused). Size the season so a player at the target daily pace finishes with time to spare ([Playio](https://blog.playio.co/battle-pass-monetization), one vendor blog, LOW). The premium unlock reuses the purchase record.
    - Bites: a late purchase grants every tier already earned; the season ends at the server `ends_at`, and unclaimed tiers auto-claim or stay claimable for a stated grace (kit default); offline progress counts in the season where the action happened; tier rewards use the key `season-pass:{account_id}:{season_id}:{track}:{tier}`; a refund revokes the premium track by the block's rule.
    - Events: `season_pass_viewed`, `season_pass_xp_credited`, `season_pass_reward_claimed`, `season_pass_premium_activated`, `season_pass_settled`.
11. **Live events** · rhythm · `engagement.event-calendar` · needs clock, an event mechanic (4 or 13)
    - Rules: three layers, recurring, feature and large-scale ([Sensor Tower, March 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/top-grossing-mobile-games-live-ops-strategies-2025-report), HIGH); the calendar is data (start, end, rules, rewards), so a new event needs no app release; daily first, weekly after the soft launch, seasonal only for a team that can ship to a calendar (guide 02); one rule per event; offset the reward inflow in the economy, as Royal Match did with a cut of roughly 30% in level-completion coins ([Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/live-ops-trends-powering-mobile-puzzle/), HIGH).
    - Bites: a client below the event's `min_app_version` sees one update row that names the event and shows no rewards; events in one `exclusive_group` may not overlap; an event ending mid-play credits the action by server time; unclaimed event rewards have a stated claim window; `ends_at` shows in local time.
    - Events: `live_event_joined` (guide 12), `live_event_hub_viewed`, `live_event_progress_recorded`, `live_event_reward_granted`, `live_event_settled`.
12. **Collections and albums** · stay · no block yet (`engagement.items` holds owned items and the duplicate rule) · needs ledger, 11, 24
    - Rules: completing a set unlocks high-value rewards and can drive pack purchases ([Sensor Tower, March 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/top-grossing-mobile-games-live-ops-strategies-2025-report), HIGH); an endless album removes the content ceiling behind boom-and-bust cycles and adds a perpetual reward inflow ([Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/live-ops-trends-powering-mobile-puzzle/), HIGH); a pack sold for money is a random reward and meets the gates of 15.
    - Bites: duplicates need a defined use (convert, trade in or cap) or they dead-end; sets added by an update leave completed sets completed; a guest merge moves or converts every owned item once.
    - Events: `item_granted`, `item_duplicate_converted`; proposed: `collection_set_completed`, `collection_pack_opened`.
13. **Leaderboards and friends** · objective · `ugc.friends-leaderboard` · needs accounts (`auth.google-login`, 17)
    - Rules: weekly and all-time boards, a player-controlled visibility switch, a private circle joined by short code; asynchronous before clans or chat (guide 04); the public-by-default choice (`PUBLIC_BOARD_DEFAULT`) is made deliberately and the board shows minimal personal data.
    - Bites: scores are device-reported and bounded, not cheat-proof; the week boundary is server time; deleting the account removes board presence (`compliance.account-deletion`); a board with few players needs a cold-start plan such as a cohort board (guide 04, no threshold known); no frozen final ranking exists for rank rewards (`engagement.event-calendar` gap).
    - Events: proposed: `leaderboard_viewed`, `friend_added`, `board_visibility_changed`.
14. **Clans and chat** · stay · no block yet · needs 13, 17, a moderation floor
    - Rules: clans work through progression (clan level), return triggers (daily donation limits, clan events) and social obligation ([Naavik, 2022](https://naavik.co/f2p-mobile/building-clan-features/), HIGH that it is claimed, MEDIUM as evidence); 4X alliances gate build help and event access, so leaving costs something ([BlueStacks](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/white-out-survival/wos-alliance-guide-en.html), MEDIUM). Floor before any free text: filter, report with timely response, block and published contact (Apple 1.2, HIGH), plus an in-app report and block system and terms acceptance before posting (Google Play UGC policy, HIGH). Chat is off when the audience includes children (interview 4, kit default).
    - Bites: a leader who stops playing needs a succession rule; leaving or removal mid-event keeps or drops contributions by a stated rule; account deletion removes or anonymises messages; message text never enters analytics; no source gives a player count below which a clan feels empty.
    - Events: proposed: `clan_joined`, `clan_left`, `clan_contributed`, `chat_message_reported`, `chat_user_blocked`.
15. **Gacha or loot boxes** · earn · no block yet (`engagement.items` offers the grant hook and disclosure fields) · needs ledger, 24, 17, age handling (interview 4)
    - Rules: the default is no paid random reward (interview 3). When yes: odds shown before purchase (Apple 3.1.1 and Google Play Payments policy, HIGH) and in the store listing; real-money prices (guide 06); a pity counter bounds the spend (guide 02, inference MEDIUM). The FTC order against a gacha publisher barred loot box sales to under-16s without parental consent and required truthful odds and a direct US-dollar purchase option ([FTC, January 2025](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/01/level-tips-businesses-ftcs-settlement-genshin-impact-developer-hoyoverse), HIGH for the terms, US). A published rate is never changed silently ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/nexon-fined-over-8-million-for-manipulating-item-drop-rates-in-games/), MEDIUM, Korean fine).
    - Bites: the server decides each pull and writes spend and grant in one transaction; each rolled item is granted through `items.grant` with its odds version; the pity counter is per account and banner and survives reinstall and a second device; a pull cut off by a kill shows its result on the next open; a refund of the pack that funded pulls follows 6; unspent tickets at banner end have a stated fate.
    - Events: `item_granted` (with `odds_version`), `odds_link_tapped`, `currency_spend`; proposed: `banner_viewed`, `pull_performed`.
16. **Push re-engagement** · rhythm · `messaging.push-reengagement` · needs 3, 5 or 11 to announce, the permission ask (guide 01)
    - Rules: ask after the first win behind an explanation screen. On Android 13 and later notifications start off and an app targeting that level chooses when the dialog shows ([Android docs](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/notification-permission), HIGH). On iOS, marketing push needs explicit in-app consent language and an in-app opt-out ([Apple 4.5.4](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH), and push may not be required for access or compensation (Apple 5.1.2(i), HIGH), so no reward for switching notifications on (reading MEDIUM). Push amplifies appointment hooks and replaces none (guide 02); every opt-in figure comes from a push vendor (LOW).
    - Bites: a scheduled push is cancelled when the player acts first (the blocks hand off `armed` and `resolved` events); quiet hours follow the account timezone; one preference per account across devices; each category has an opt-out in settings; a denied permission is re-asked only from a settings entry or a feature that needs it.
    - Events: `push_priming_shown`, `push_system_prompt_result`, `push_category_toggled`, `push_sent`, `push_opened` (guide 12), `comeback_granted`.
17. **Sign-in and cloud save** · safety · `auth.google-login`, `auth.sign-in-with-apple`, `data.progress-sync`, `compliance.account-deletion` · needs 25
    - Rules: link a provider to the guest account so the `account_id` stays; an app offering a third-party login for the primary account must also offer an equivalent login that limits data collection ([Apple 4.8](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH), which is why `auth.sign-in-with-apple` accompanies `auth.google-login`; account creation brings in-app deletion (Apple 5.1.1(v), HIGH); interview 5 defaults to progress on the device only.
    - Bites: the client snapshot carries local progress only, and server-owned state (ledger, streak, energy, objectives, queues, items) never enters it and merges through each block's merge hook (`mergeAccounts` or `account_merged`); a different account on a shared device gets its own progress and the previous data is kept aside; purchases made as a guest follow the `account_id`; deletion removes board presence, tokens and personal data in every block's eraser.
    - Events: `account_link_started`, `account_linked`, `link_conflict_resolved`, `sign_in_failed` (`auth.guest-account`); `data.progress-sync` and `compliance.account-deletion` define none.
18. **Settings** · safety · no block yet (`compliance.account-deletion` and `billing.in-app-purchase` supply entries) · needs 17
    - Rules: sound, haptics, language, notification switches per category, a privacy policy link inside the app ([Apple 5.1.1(i)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH), account actions (link, sign out, delete), a visible Restore purchases (Apple 3.1.1, HIGH), a support entry (20), the app version and player id; consent choices can be reopened.
    - Bites: volume and haptics live on the device, notification choices on the account; signing out never wipes guest progress; deletion asks for confirmation and re-authentication (`compliance.account-deletion`).
    - Events: `push_category_toggled` (`messaging.push-reengagement`); proposed: `setting_changed`, `restore_purchases_tapped`.
19. **Rating prompt** · reach · no block yet · needs 3, a session counter
    - Rules: the system prompt only. StoreKit shows it at most three times in a rolling year, may show nothing, and is not called in response to a button tap ([Apple StoreKit docs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/requestreviewaction), HIGH). Google Play enforces a time-bound quota and says to use no trigger button and to ask no question before the prompt, such as "Do you like the app?" ([Android docs](https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-review), HIGH). A rating is never required for access (Apple 3.2.2, HIGH). Trigger after a win and several sessions, never in the tutorial (guide 01); a "Rate us" row in settings opens the store page.
    - Bites: the API reports no result, so log requests and not ratings; skip the prompt after a failure, a declined purchase or a support contact; keep one request state per app version and platform.
    - Events: proposed: `rating_prompt_requested`, `rating_store_page_opened`.
20. **Support** · safety · no block yet · needs 18
    - Rules: an easy contact path in the app and on the store Support URL ([Apple 1.5](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH); the form attaches account id, app version, platform and a short client log (kit default); purchase questions start with Restore purchases, and store refunds reach the game as notifications (6).
    - Bites: a player without an account quotes the player id from settings; a purchase complaint is found by `store_transaction_id`; chat reports go to moderation, not support.
    - Events: proposed: `support_opened`, `support_request_sent`.
21. **Referral or invite** · reach · no block yet (`ugc.friends-leaderboard` has a friend code) · needs 25, ledger
    - Rules: share through the system share sheet or a code, since Apple steers away from full Contacts access ([Apple 5.1.1(iii)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), HIGH) and bars contacting people from Contacts except at the user's explicit, individualized initiative and bars a Select All option (Apple 5.1.2(v), HIGH); an invite is not core functionality and never gates play (Apple 5.1.1(v), HIGH); pay both sides when the invitee finishes the tutorial, not on install (kit default). A realistic viral coefficient is 0.3 to 0.5, a modest acquisition discount ([Echai Ventures](https://echai.ventures/startingup/retention-referral/what-s-a-viral-coefficient-and-is-chasing-a-k-factor-above-1-realistic-for-my-kind-of-product), MEDIUM, generic app blogs).
    - Bites: one reward per invitee account (`referral:{inviter_account_id}:{invitee_account_id}:{line_no}`); guest accounts are cheap to farm, so pay on tutorial completion and check self-invites and duplicates on the server (`auth.guest-account`); an invitee who already has the game earns no reward; off when the audience includes children (interview 4).
    - Events: proposed: `invite_sent`, `invite_accepted`, `invite_reward_granted`.
22. **Force update and maintenance** · safety · no block yet · needs a version endpoint
    - Rules: the server holds `min_supported_version` and `recommended_version` per platform, checked at start and resume: below the minimum a blocking screen with the store link, below the recommended a dismissible nudge (guide 12 rule 20); maintenance adds `ends_at` and a message (kit default). Android's in-app updates API offers a flexible flow and an immediate full-screen flow for updates "critical to the core functionality" ([Android docs](https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-updates), HIGH); no Apple rule or API for a forced update was found (Gaps), so iOS opens the store page.
    - Bites: a failed check fails open, so an offline player is locked out only when the last known response set a floor above the installed version; maintenance blocks purchases and claims on the server as well as in the UI and counts down to `ends_at`; the store link is per platform.
    - Events: proposed: `update_prompt_shown`, `update_accepted`, `maintenance_shown`.
23. **Timed queues** · rhythm · `engagement.timed-queues` · needs ledger, clock, purchase contract (paid slots)
    - Rules: slots hold one item at a time and chain; durations, costs and outputs are data; a speed-up is priced from remaining time and quoted before the spend; the free path and a free short remainder always exist; no paid skip for flagged minors by default. Supercell tested instant and day-long chests with total reward value held constant ([Supercell, Jan 2025](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/news/chest-experiment-king-tower-levels-1-9), HIGH), and no source measures the retention or revenue effect of timers or speed-ups (evidence section of `engagement.timed-queues`; guide 02 Gaps).
    - Bites: the device clock changes nothing; two devices start once and the second learns why; a start, speed-up or collect cut off by a crash completes or is compensated once; a config edit never lengthens a running item; a refunded slot purchase stops new starts in that slot and never destroys running work; a notification can fire for an item sped up on another device.
    - Events: `queue_item_started`, `queue_item_ready`, `queue_item_collected`, `queue_item_cancelled`, `queue_speedup_quoted`, `queue_speedup_applied`, `queue_slot_unlocked`, `queue_idle_nudge_shown`.
24. **Items and inventory** · stay · `engagement.items` · needs ledger, 25 (merge); random sources call `items.grant`
    - Rules: a code is a `count` (a ledger balance) or an `instance` (a row with level, attributes, expiry), never both, and interchangeable items stay ledger counts; every duplicate has a defined use (convert, trade in or cap; cap never on a random source, INV-7); a full bag holds the grant with a claim path and never destroys a reward (INV-14, guide 14 rule 12); a purchased item does not expire in the inventory (INV-17; Apple 3.1.1 covers purchased currency, HIGH); no item required for progress is sold only for money (INV-22).
    - Bites: an upgrade cut off between spend and level either completes or returns the materials once (INV-8); a changed cost returns a new quote and spends nothing (INV-12); a stale `expected_version` is rejected (INV-13); expiry follows the server clock and converts or warns first (INV-15, INV-16); a retired item stays owned (INV-18); a guest merge moves or converts every instance once (INV-20).
    - Events: `item_granted`, `item_upgraded`, `item_equipped`, `item_consumed`, `item_converted`, `item_expiring_soon`, `item_expired`, `items_merged`, `inventory_viewed`.
25. **Guest account** · safety · `auth.guest-account` · needs nothing; every block keyed by `account_id` needs it
    - Rules: the server issues the `account_id` silently on first launch, with no screen or prompt, and the device holds a secret; linking keeps the same `account_id`, so no row of any block is re-keyed (INV-7); offer linking once, after the first win, before the first purchase or from settings, and "Not now" costs nothing; a guest identity lives on the device, so the game says so and offers a recovery code; Apple's "let people use it without a login" applies (5.1.1(v), HIGH); `AUDIENCE_MODE` `kids` issues an internal identifier and shows no link prompt.
    - Bites: creation is rate-limited and idempotent per request id, so a lost response leaves no orphan; an offline first launch plays with identity pending and submits its queued events later; linking to a credential owned by another account opens a sheet that lists what each choice keeps and removes, and balances and entitlements never merge silently (INV-9, INV-10); a dormant guest with paid holdings is never purged (INV-14); a guest can delete from inside the app (INV-15).
    - Events: `guest_created`, `link_prompt_shown`, `account_link_started`, `account_linked`, `link_conflict_resolved`, `guest_recovered`.

## Default path

1. `auth.guest-account` (25), the event pipeline (guide 12), settings (18), support (20) and the version gate (22). This shell carries every later feature: nothing keyed by `account_id` runs before the guest account exists, and the version gate reaches players on an old build.
2. The ledger and the purchase record from `billing.virtual-currency`, before any feature that pays out (1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 15, 21, 23, 24). Items and inventory (24) follow it when the game owns objects with their own level, expiry or duplicates; interchangeable items stay ledger counts.
3. Core loop, level progression, tutorial and first win (2, 1). The tutorial gift and the reward per cycle both use the ledger.
4. Daily reward and streak, starter and daily objectives (3, 4): the first return reason, `engagement.daily-streak` and `engagement.objectives`.
5. Energy or lives where the archetype uses it (5), timed queues where it has waits (23), then rewarded ads (8): the ads meet the same blocked moment and credit through the ledger or `grantEnergy`.
6. Sign-in and cloud save (17) before the first purchase surface: `auth.google-login` with `auth.sign-in-with-apple`, then `data.progress-sync`. `compliance.account-deletion` ships with the first sign-in, because account creation requires in-app deletion. Skip the step when interview 5 says progress stays on the device.
7. The shop, starter pack and offers (6, 7): they need the ledger, the purchase record and a store sandbox pass.
8. Push re-engagement (16), once a daily reward or a timer exists to announce, and the rating prompt (19), after the first win.
9. Soft launch (guide 05). Its gates decide steps 10 to 13.
10. Event calendar and live events (11), the weekly layer first, then the season pass (10), which needs the calendar, a progress source from objectives or events, and the purchase record.
11. Subscription or VIP (9): needs `billing.virtual-currency` (purchase record and store receivers), the entitlement contract and enough ongoing value to qualify under Apple 3.1.2(a).
12. Collections (12), leaderboards and friends (13) and referral (21), then clans (14) after friends and accounts, and chat last, only behind the moderation floor.
13. Gacha (15) only when interview 3 allows paid random rewards, after the ledger, items, accounts, age handling and odds disclosure exist.

## Gaps

- No source gives a measured retention or revenue lift for any feature in this guide; the only streak experiment is Duolingo's, and guides 02 and 04 hold the rest of the negative results. Levels, XP curves, unlock cadence, skill trees and achievements returned only low-quality sources (guide 02 Gaps), so guide 08 treats them as design judgement. No tunable has a sourced value: tutorial length, energy cap and regeneration, reward calendar length, season length, clan size, invite reward size; the Candy Crush figure is a fan wiki example, not a norm.
- The Apple guideline text was read through Guideline 5.2.5 (the first 100,000 of 114,428 characters), so a forced-update rule or a gambling rule in the unread tail would not appear here. Google Play pages were read through a summarizing fetch, and their quotes are not checked against the rendered page.
- The game-side response to a refund of spent currency has no source; the stores document the notification, and `billing.virtual-currency` picks the rule. No source covers idle-game social features, maintenance-mode design, guest-account loss rates or the cold-start size of a clan, so cells marked † in the matrix rest on judgement alone.
- Events listed after "proposed" have no owning block or guide 12 row. All others were checked against the block READMEs and guide 12 on 2026-10-27; `compliance.account-deletion` and `data.progress-sync` define no analytics events.

## Checklist

- [ ] Every feature in the game has its Rules, Bites and Events written, and the Bites are covered by a test or a scenario.
- [ ] Every currency and item reward goes through the ledger with an idempotency key and a registered reason; energy goes through `grantEnergy`; no feature writes a balance directly.
- [ ] No timer, window or day boundary is decided by the device clock, and the matrix row for each feature matches the archetype and the interview answers, with an owner testing each † cell.
- [ ] Odds disclosure, purchased-currency persistence, restore, in-app deletion and the chat floor are met wherever a feature triggers them.
- [ ] The build order was followed: the guest account and the ledger before payouts, accounts before cloud save, the moderation floor before chat.
- [ ] Each screen that carries a feature is listed in guide 10, and each event is in guide 12's schema.
